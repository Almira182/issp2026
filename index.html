Dedicated to the 95th anniversary of Academician E.A. Bekturov and the 75th anniversary of Prof. S. Kudaibergenov

7–9 October 2026
Al-Farabi Kazakh National University

About the event

SYMPOSIUM TOPICS

01

Synthesis, modification and characterization of specialty polymers

02

Functional, stimuli-responsive and smart polymeric materials

03

Polymers for biomedical, pharmaceutical and drug-delivery applications

04

Polymers for environmental protection, water treatment and enhanced oil recovery

05

Specialty polymers for energy storage, catalysis, membranes and coatings

06

Polymer colloids, interfaces, self-assembly, supramolecular systems, scale-up and commercialization

Abstract submission

Abstract requirements

Please prepare your abstract according to the formatting rules below. The abstract should fit on one page.

  • ✓
    Title

    Capital letters, Times New Roman, 12 pt, centered alignment.

  • ✓
    Authors

    Times New Roman, 12 pt, centered alignment.

  • ✓
    Affiliation and contact details

    Organization, city, country and e-mail; Times New Roman, 12 pt, centered alignment.

  • ✓
    Main text

    Times New Roman, 12 pt, justified alignment, single line spacing, 20 mm margins and 1.25 cm first-line indentation.

  • ✓
    References

    Include all cited literature at the end of the abstract.
